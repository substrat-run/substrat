The desk's top bar ends in an account menu rather than three loose pieces of one.
It used to print the signed-in name, then the avatar of the same person, then a
button marked "Switch" — read left to right that is a label, a picture and an
unexplained verb, and none of them says "this is you". The avatar is now the
control: clicking it opens a menu that names who is signed in, offers "Switch
user" (the persona swap at the issuer, which is what "Switch" meant), and offers
"Sign out". Signing out had no way in at all before this — the route existed and
nothing in the app reached it, so the only way to stop being signed in was to
clear the cookie by hand. The menu closes on a click outside or Escape, like the
notifications tray beside it.
