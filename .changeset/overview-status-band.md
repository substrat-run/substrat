An app's Overview now opens with a status band: schedule health, the error rate over the last day, the version it is running and whether an update is waiting, and whether anyone has claimed its owner seat. Under the app's address, a 24-hour traffic sparkline with its deploys marked on it. Each stat links to the page that explains it, so the answer to "is this app OK?" is on the app's own page and only the deep dive is a click away.
