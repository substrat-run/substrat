An app's page in the dashboard is now four tabs — Overview, Deployments, Data and Settings — instead of one tab per feature. Schema and Previews moved under Data, beside the tables they describe and copy; Permissions moved under Settings, where the rest of an app's read-and-configure surfaces live; the update comparison and schema history moved to Deployments, next to the Update button they answer for. The audit log left the app page for its own entry in the left menu, where it covers every app on the team at once and carries a filter for narrowing to one — the app's Overview links straight into it, already narrowed. Existing links to the old tabs keep working.
