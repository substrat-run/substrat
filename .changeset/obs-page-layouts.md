Pulse, Processes and Logs each have their own layout now. Pulse shows every app on one clock — requests, error rate, p95, a sparkline and its health verdict per row — and, narrowed to one app, adds its releases and its schedules on the same axis. Logs opens on a filter bar where each filter is a removable chip, and its lines are a dense, colour-coded stream you can open to see every field, where clicking a value filters by it. Processes drops the time controls it never used.
