Observability is now a page in the dashboard's left menu, covering every app on your team
rather than one app at a time. Traffic over time for every installation is drawn on a
single chart with one line each, one time range governs the whole page, and the app filter
narrows it to one app — its traffic per surface, its logs, its events, its schedules and
its flow map, all on that same time axis.

The per-app Observability tab and the old preview Analytics page both lead here, and links
to either keep working: an app's tab opens the page already filtered to that app, and a
flow map's event link opens the event explorer already grouped on that type. An app's
Overview has an "Open in Observability" link beside its audit log one.

If your team publishes the vertical an app runs, the traffic view links up to the vertical
for the fleet numbers across everyone who installed it — an app page now answers about
that one installation, for its publisher too.
