Todo may call Notes as the person signed in to it, with the notepad's keys only (`delegations` in the manifest, the `peer` channel). Item notes have keys of their own, `item-note:read` and `item-note:write`, which `writer` holds and the delegation does not, because their operations rely on the door's vouch from Todo (#3161).
