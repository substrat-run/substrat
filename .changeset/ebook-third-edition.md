A third edition of the ebook, reviewing weeks 42–46 and the merges of 16–20 November. Two new
chapters: apps made of other apps (peer calls and exported events, a plugin that takes its people
from another app, a call made as the signed-in person, an app that creates tenants, and where the
module and vertical line falls) and screens (the published web components, the generated client
and files served as the person). The chapters from the old chapter 8 onwards move up by two.
Existing chapters cover the worker door, verified addresses, request ids, reserved keys, the
migration data-loss fence, platform-built releases, stores, connectors a vertical ships, the Logs
view and daily usage capture. The cover and EPUB title page say fifteen chapters.
