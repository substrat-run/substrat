`<sub-kanban>`: a column's `colour` is drawn before its label; `sortable="false"` is for a board that keeps no order within a column; every word it shows or announces is replaceable (`labels`) and translatable through Web Awesome's registry (`registerKanbanLabels`); `--sub-kanban-column-grow` lets columns fill a wide board.
