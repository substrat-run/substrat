A pending team invite can be copied from the Team page. Every invited row now has a **Copy link** action that shows and copies that invite's accept link without emailing the invitee again (a lapsed invitation is renewed on the way). The invite dialog also no longer says email delivery is coming: it says whether the email was sent.
