A pending team invite can be copied from the Team page. Every invited row now has a **Copy link** action that shows and copies that invite's accept link without emailing the invitee again. It is a pure read: a lapsed invitation is refused with a pointer to Resend, and the dialog shows the invite's real expiry. The invite dialog also no longer says email delivery is coming: it says whether the email was sent.
