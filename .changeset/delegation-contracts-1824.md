New delegation vocabulary for token exchange at a team auth server: `DELEGATIONS_CONFIG_PREFIX` (`substrat:delegations:<host app scope id>`), the `delegationGrant` / `delegationGrants` schemas for its value (each actor app with the host permissions it may act with), and the RFC 8693 constants `TOKEN_EXCHANGE_GRANT_TYPE` and `TOKEN_TYPE`.
