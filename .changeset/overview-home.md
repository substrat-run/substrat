The dashboard's Overview is now a home page of its own instead of a second copy of the Apps list. It opens with one plain sentence about how your apps and integrations are doing, saying whether a problem is on the app's side or the supplier's, followed by a list of everything that needs attention with a link to where to fix it. Below that are your app cards, each with its health in words and an Observe link, then your integrations and the latest entries from the audit log. If one of these can't be loaded, only that section says so and the rest of the page still works.
