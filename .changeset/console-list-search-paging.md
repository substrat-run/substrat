Tenants, Domains and Verticals get a search box and the Scopes pager (25, 50 or 100 per page). Tenants hide reaped tombstones by default, with a status filter to bring them back; Domains can be filtered by hostname status. Domains and Verticals now filter and page over the full list in the browser instead of loading it a cursor page at a time.
