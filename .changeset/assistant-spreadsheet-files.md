Support private CSV, TSV and XLSX inputs in assistant conversations. Hosts can upload and read files through a thread-authorized broker, and messages retain bounded, immutable file references. A structured spreadsheet reader preserves rows, columns, identifiers and decimal spelling for import previews and comparisons, with explicit formula-cache warnings and refusal rather than truncation when limits are exceeded.
