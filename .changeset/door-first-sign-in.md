`workerDoor` joins a person the first time they reach a scope, through the `join` a worker names, and then gives them every shape its `permissions` declare `bootstrap` with `holder: 'self'`, once, so a revoke stays revoked (`grantSelfShapesLocal`, `grantEntityShapeOnceIn`). A hosted owner could sign in and then not create anything. The template's worker says where a join goes.
