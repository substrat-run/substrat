## Information we collect and why

- **Account information.** When you create an account or sign in, we receive the
  information needed to identify you, such as your email address, name, profile
  picture, and sign-in provider identifier. We use it to authenticate you, manage
  your account and team membership, and communicate about the service.
- **Information you submit.** This includes data you store on the platform,
  support messages, and feedback. We process it to provide the features you use
  and respond to your requests. Support messages may be processed by an AI
  assistant to answer questions or help route them to a person.
- **Email signups.** If you join the beta waiting list or subscribe to updates,
  we collect your email address and any optional note. Signups require email
  confirmation. You can unsubscribe using the link in an update or ask support
  to remove your signup.
- **Technical information.** Hosting and service systems process information
  such as IP addresses, browser details, request logs, timestamps, and usage
  records to deliver the service, diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and keep
  accounts secure.

## Google sign-in

When you choose Google sign-in, we request basic identity information: your
Google account identifier, email address, and profile information. We use this
information to sign you in and associate you with your Substrat account. Google
sign-in does not give us access to your Gmail messages, Drive files, or contacts.
We do not use Google sign-in information for advertising or sell it.
You can revoke access in your [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
Revoking access does not by itself delete your Substrat account; contact support
to request deletion.

## Cookies and browser storage

Hosted applications use cookies or similar storage to maintain sign-in sessions
and security. The support widget uses browser storage to remember your
conversation between visits. You can clear or block storage in your browser;
doing so may sign you out or reset the widget's conversation history. Clearing
browser storage does not delete information already submitted to the service.

## Sharing and service providers

We use service providers for hosting, authentication, email delivery, and AI
features. These include Cloudflare for hosting and infrastructure, Resend for
support desk email delivery, and AI providers such as Anthropic when selected
to answer support messages. Google processes sign-in information when you choose
Google sign-in. Providers process the information needed to perform those functions.
Information you put in a team or application may also be available to its
authorized members and administrators. We may disclose information when required
by law or necessary to investigate abuse, protect the service, or protect people's
rights and safety. We do not sell personal information.

Providers, including Cloudflare, may process information in the United States
and other countries outside your own. For transfers from the European Economic
Area, applicable safeguards include the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the
recipient is certified and the transfer is covered, or the European Commission's
Standard Contractual Clauses. Cloudflare describes its transfer safeguards in its
[Data Processing Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/).
You can contact us to request information about the safeguards applicable to
your data.

## Retention and security

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the service,
maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention
depends on the type of information and its purpose; backups and required records
may remain after account deletion for a limited period. We use technical and
organizational measures to protect information, but no service can guarantee
absolute security.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export,
delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, object to processing,
or withdraw consent. Where applicable, we process information to provide a
service you request, pursue legitimate interests such as security and support,
comply with legal obligations, or with your consent. You may also complain to
your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, email [info@substrat.net](mailto:info@substrat.net) or
contact our [support desk](/guide/support). We may need
to verify your identity. If your information is managed by an organization using
Substrat, contact that organization first; it controls how its application uses
your data.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will post the current
version here, update the date above, and provide additional notice where required.
