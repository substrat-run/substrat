- **Who uses it:** me and my family.
- **What a task is:** it moves through stages, has an owner and a due date, and
  holds a checklist of steps inside it.
- **Screens:** a web UI — a board or list I can click around in.
- **Who sees what:** lists are private until you share them with specific family
  members.
- **Assigning:** you can only add tasks to the lists you have access to, and only
  assign them to the people on that list.
- **What a share allows:** full control of the list — rename it, delete it, and
  choose who else is on it.
- **Who creates lists:** anyone in the household can create a list and share it
  with whoever they want.
