# D-17 — Document product's engine extracts into the kernel

Document product's engine **extracts into** the kernel; the product re-platforms onto the kernel piecemeal as consumer #2 — not integrated as an external document service

## Why

One permission regime over documents (two = the leak §4 prevents); proves contracts on a second shape; extraction timed by case-1 need, re-platforming opportunistic to avoid a third greenfield (§6, risk 4)
